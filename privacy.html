Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026. Applies to the HomeCubbie mobile app and this website.

The short version. HomeCubbie collects nothing about you. There is no account to create, no server to send anything to, and no analytics of any kind. Everything you enter is stored on your own device and never transmitted.

What HomeCubbie stores, and where

The app keeps a database file and a documents folder in its own private storage on your device. That is where your Property, Places, Things, Tasks, Policies, Events, Notes, People and Accounts live, along with any photos you attach.

Nobody but you and the apps you explicitly share a file with can read that storage. The developer cannot. There is no mechanism by which the developer could — no server receives it, and the app makes no network requests.

What is never collected

  • Personal information. No name, email address, phone number, or postal address is requested or transmitted.
  • Usage and analytics data. No screen views, no session counts, no feature tracking, no crash reports.
  • Identifiers. No advertising identifier, no device identifier, no cookies in the app.
  • Location. The app does not request location access.
  • Contacts, calendars, health data, or your photo library. None of these are requested.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, or hand to a third party, and no data broker relationship exists.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

Camera

Only if you choose to photograph a document or a serial plate. Images are written to the app's private storage on your device. The photo library itself is not accessed.

Face ID, Touch ID, or device biometrics

Only if you turn on the app lock. HomeCubbie asks the operating system whether the person holding the phone is the one enrolled on it, and receives a yes or a no. No biometric data is ever seen, received, or stored by the app — it never leaves the secure hardware on your device.

Notifications

Only if you allow reminders. These are local notifications scheduled with your device's operating system. HomeCubbie sends no push notifications and has no capability to; there is no push server, and the remote-push entitlement is deliberately removed from the shipped app.

Backups you create

HomeCubbie can export a backup file containing your data and pass it to your device's share sheet. You choose the destination — another app, a file on the device, a cloud drive of your own. Once you send that file somewhere, the privacy policy of wherever you sent it applies to it, so choose a destination you trust.

This is the only way data leaves the app, and it happens only when you ask for it.

Automatic operating-system backups are switched off

By default, mobile operating systems copy an app's private storage into the user's cloud backup. HomeCubbie deliberately opts out on both platforms, so your records are not swept into a cloud account without your asking. On iOS the app's data is additionally marked for the strongest file-protection class available, meaning it is encrypted and unreadable whenever the device is locked.

Deleting your data

Delete an entry inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything at once. Because nothing was ever transmitted, there is no copy elsewhere to request the deletion of, and no account to close.

Children

HomeCubbie is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

This website

homecubbie.com is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party fonts, scripts, or embeds — the one typeface it uses is served from this site itself.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser user-agent in the ordinary course of serving a page to you, and this is governed by GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing further is recorded, and no visitor information reaches the developer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Any change that would alter what the app collects would first require the app itself to change, and the guarantee above is enforced by the app's own automated tests, not merely stated here.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything else: support@homecubbie.com.
