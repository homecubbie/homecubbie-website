Common questions

Does HomeCubbie sync between my devices?

No, and it is not planned. Syncing requires a server holding your data, which is the thing HomeCubbie is built to avoid. Your records live on one device.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup file from Settings on the old phone, get that file to the new phone however you like, install HomeCubbie there, and restore from it. The backup contains everything you have entered.

What happens if I lose my phone?

If you have a backup file, restore it on the new device. If you do not, the records are gone — there is no copy on a server, because there is no server. This is the honest trade for having no account and no cloud, and it is worth taking a backup now rather than later.

Why does it not appear in my iCloud or Google backup?

Deliberately. Automatic operating-system backups would copy alarm codes, policy numbers and account details into a cloud account over the network, which contradicts the one promise the app makes. HomeCubbie opts out on both platforms, so the backup file you export yourself is the way to keep a copy.

I forgot to allow notifications and now reminders do not appear.

Reminders need notification permission from the operating system. Turn it on in your device's Settings under HomeCubbie, and scheduled Tasks will start surfacing again.

Can I use it for more than one property?

The app is built around one Property as the root of everything. If you need more, say so in an email — knowing what people actually want is how the next version gets decided.

Is my data encrypted?

On iOS the app's storage is marked for the strongest file-protection class the system offers, so it is encrypted and unreadable whenever the device is locked. You can also require Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode to open the app at all — see the app lock in Settings.

Reporting a bug

Useful things to include, in whatever detail you have:

  • What you were doing when it happened, step by step if you can.
  • What you expected instead.
  • Your device model and OS version.
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of Settings.

Please do not send screenshots containing alarm codes, policy numbers or account numbers. Blur them, or describe the screen instead.
